How to play

Be the first to play every tile from your rack.

Sets

  • Run — 3+ consecutive numbers in the same color, like 456
  • Group — 3 or 4 of the same number in different colors, like 999
  • Jokers ☺ stand in for any tile.

Your turn

  • Your first play (the initial meld) must be worth 30+ points using only your own tiles.
  • After that you can add to and rearrange any sets on the table — as long as every set is valid when you end your turn.
  • Can’t (or don’t want to) play? Draw a tile and your turn ends.

Controls

  • Drag tiles onto a set, onto the empty table to start a new set, or back to your rack (only tiles you played this turn).
  • Tap tiles to select several, then tap a set, the + New set spot, or your rack to move them all.
  • Undo steps back one move. Reset undoes your whole turn.

Scoring

When someone goes out, everyone else loses the value of their remaining tiles (jokers are worth 30) and the winner collects the total.
